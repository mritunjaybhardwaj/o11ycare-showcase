What this result means

The automated test checks sequence identities, not just total counts: duplicate records cannot hide a missing sequence. It also checks Loki stream labels and event tenant fields. The two tenant workloads overlapped, and query separation was checked in both directions.

This validates the recorded dispatcher-outage scenario. It does not establish exactly-once delivery, unlimited retention, authentication, noisy-neighbor isolation, or resilience to disk-full and pod/node-loss conditions.
